## Kubernetes / Releases

### etcd v3.7.0 released
SIG etcd released etcd v3.7.0, the latest minor version of the distributed key-value store at the heart of every Kubernetes control plane. The release ships the long-requested RangeStream feature and other performance improvements, removes the last remnants of the legacy v2store, and completes a major protobuf overhaul.

- Source: [Announcing etcd v3.7.0](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2026/07/08/announcing-etcd-3.7/) (Kubernetes Blog, Jul 8)
- Related page: [Cluster Architecture](../core/01-cluster-architecture.md)

The third alpha of the upcoming v1.37 release is out. Alphas are not for production use, but they offer an early look at what's landing in the next minor version.

- Source: [v1.37.0-alpha.3 release notes](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/releases/tag/v1.37.0-alpha.3) (GitHub, Jul 8)

## Amazon EKS

### EKS Auto Mode cuts GPU management fees by up to 60%
Amazon EKS Auto Mode reduced its management fees for GPU instances by up to 60%. The pricing update applies in all AWS Regions where EKS Auto Mode is available, and Amazon ECS Managed Instances gets an identical reduction. This meaningfully lowers the cost of running AI/ML workloads on Auto Mode.

- Source: [Amazon EKS Auto Mode reduces GPU management fees by up to 60%](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/07/amazon-eks-auto-mode-gpu-price) (AWS What's New, Jul 7)
- Related page: [EKS Auto Mode Cost Management](../eks-auto-mode/06-cost-management.md)

## Observability

### CloudWatch Application Signals auto-captures errors, anomalies, and deployments
CloudWatch Application Signals added Service Events, which automatically captures exception snapshots, latency anomalies, and deployment events. It works out of the box for applications instrumented with the ADOT SDKs or the CloudWatch Observability EKS add-on, with Java, Python, and JavaScript supported.

- Source: [CloudWatch Application Signals now automatically captures errors, performance anomalies, and deployment events](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/06/cloudwatch-service-events/) (AWS What's New, Jul 6)
- Related page: [CloudWatch Metrics](../observability/metrics/04-cloudwatch-metrics.md)

## AI/ML & Security

### Why sandboxing your agent is not enough
A CNCF blog post argues that runtime sandboxing alone doesn't secure AI agents: once an agent interacts with external tools and networks, you need defense in depth — least-privilege credentials, network policies, and more — on top of isolation.

- Source: [Why sandboxing your agent is not enough](https://www.cncf.io/blog/2026/07/07/why-sandboxing-your-agent-is-not-enough/) (CNCF Blog, Jul 7)
